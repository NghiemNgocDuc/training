== OBJECTIVE ==
Fine-tune MACE-OFF23 (a foundation model for molecules) on FreeSolv 
(642 small organic molecules) to predict solvation free energy (ΔG_solv 
in kcal/mol). Beat SOTA MAE 0.417 (Zhang 2022).

Input: 3D geometry (atomic numbers + coordinates) → Output: scalar ΔG per molecule.

All ΔG in kcal/mol (1 kcal/mol = 4.184 kJ/mol).
Typical FreeSolv experimental uncertainty: ~0.1-0.3 kcal/mol.

Metric reference:
  MAE (mean absolute error) = avg |prediction − experiment|
    <0.4 beats SOTA | 0.52 = DimeNet++ FT | 3.0 = zero-shot | 20 = useless (GBn2)
  RMSE = sqrt(avg(pred−exp)²) — penalizes outliers
    0.72 = Zhang SOTA | 0.84 = DimeNet++ FT
  R² = fraction of variance explained
    1.0 = perfect | 0.96 = excellent | 0.0 = mean baseline

What 0.4 kcal/mol looks like physically:
  ~kT at room temp (0.6). Typical H-bond = 1.0. Drug discovery: <1.0 useful for
  ranking, <0.5 for quantitative design.

== KEY FINDINGS FROM Zhang 2026 (npj Comput Mater 12, 174) ==

Paper: "Constructing MLIPs with minimum ab initio data" — Wang group at CAS.
Directly relevant to our MACE-OFF23 FreeSolv work.

Core workflow (3 stages):
  Stage 0: Run HT MD with pretrained MACE-MP-0 directly (no fine-tuning) to
           generate trajectories. DIRECT resampling (PCA + stratified clustering
           on M3GNet features) picks ~200 diverse configs for one-shot DFT
           labeling. Key justification: MACE-MP-0 trajectories have ~90% PCA
           feature overlap with AIMD despite 22.8% RRMSE on transition energies,
           so it's good enough to sample with but not accurate enough to trust.
  Stage 1: Fine-tune MACE-MP-0 on ~200 DFT configs (full FT or ELoRA).
  Stage 2: Distill to lightweight NEP (teacher labels 2000 pseudo-configs,
           zero additional DFT). NEP recovers most accuracy with 20× speedup.

Key findings relevant to us:
  - MD instability mechanism: ZBL alone does NOT fix instability (Fig. S7).
    Root cause is PES SMOOTHNESS, not just short-range repulsive trend.
    A model can have correct repulsive energy trend at short r, but an unsmooth
    ("kinked") energy curve — slope changes abruptly, producing spurious
    attractive forces that destabilise MD.
    Pretrained/fine-tuned models inherit smoothness from pretrained PES,
    avoiding this problem entirely.
    Fine-tuning with just 10 configs yields stable MD; training from scratch
    on 800 configs is still unstable.
    Diagnosis: check diatomic energy curves for kinks, not just ZBL sign.
  - ELoRA matches full fine-tuning accuracy with far fewer trainable params.
  - Single-shot sampling + fine-tuning replaces costly active learning loops.
  - Distilled NEP: 17% lower energy RMSE, 11% lower force RMSE than NEP
    trained on 200 DFT configs (because teacher provides 2000 labels vs 200).
  - Long-range electrostatics still a known gap in current uMLIPs.

Relevance to FreeSolv:
  - MACE-OFF23 is pretrained on SPICE v1 (wB97M+D3, exactly the organic
    molecules in FreeSolv). Fine-tuning on 642 labels should inherit smooth
    PES and reach sub-0.4 MAE — directly parallels their Stage 1.
  - No need for Stage 0 sampling — FreeSolv labels already exist.
  - Distillation (Stage 2) not needed here — we just need per-molecule ΔG,
    not fast MD. But if we wanted to run MD with our model, same principles apply.
