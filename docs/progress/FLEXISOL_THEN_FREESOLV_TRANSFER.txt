Pipeline expectation: train on FlexiSol-water first, then fine-tune on FreeSolv
Status: scoping (not implemented)
Date: 2026-08-07

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Setup
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two-step transfer, SAME task (both are experimental aqueous dG-solv in
kcal/mol at 298.15 K; same units, same solvent).

  1. init:             stage2_correction.pt  (the AQM pipeline correction ckpt)
  2. step A:           tune FlexiSol-water  (239 train mols), save flexisol_tuned.pt
  3. step B (continue): flexisol_tuned.pt -> FreeSolv fold-0 (411 train), TTA eval

Baselines to compare against:
  - direct FreeSolv fine-tune   ~0.550 TTA (0.531 single)  [fair control]
  - frozen stage2 member        -> 0.5048 TTA  (goal to approach/beat)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Expected outcome (honest)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Same ballpark band: ~0.505-0.55 TTA, most likely within noise of direct
  FreeSolv fine-tune. Reasons: same-task transfer, and FlexiSol's 239 mols is
  a small add-on on top of FreeSolv 411.
- No theory mismatch and no polarity mismatch (no AQM-style gap here; both
  datasets are experimental) -> catastrophic forgetting low, gain bounded.
- Two things the experiment can actually catch:
   * label compilation drift between Flexi vs FreeSolv -> small negative shift
   * halogen-ish / low-coverage subset gain in FreeSol observationiles that
     FlexSol already over-covers -> small positive effect on a subset table.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it will NOT do
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- It will not "create signal": previous experiments (approaches 0/1/1m/3) show
  disagreement/labels do not buy accuracy above the 0.50 TTA band. A second
  fine-tune is a warm-start benefit (a few %) at best, not a new source of
  information.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Deliverables (if implemented)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- per-step checkpoints: stageA_flexisol.pt, stageB_freesolv.pt
- report: FreeSolv fold-0 test MAE/RMSE single + TTA for stageB,
  plus subset table (F/Cl/Br region) computed by the existing subset_report.
- compare log + tail # save figures.
